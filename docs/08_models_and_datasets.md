## Inspection recipe before real training

1. Read the pinned dataset card and license.
2. Inspect a few episodes without loading the entire archive.
3. Check timestamp order, action latency, camera views, dropped frames, units, and normalization.
4. Plot action histograms and state trajectories; detect duplicate or failed episodes.
5. Split by episode, scene, session, and relevant domain shift.
6. Retain the raw data revision and preprocessing manifest.

A passive video corpus can train visual prediction without robot commands. Action-conditioned dynamics needs actual transition-aligned interventions, or an explicitly stated latent-action inference method whose controls require later grounding.

# 10. Applications and commercial relevance

Previous: [engineering](09_training_and_inference.md). Next: [research directions](11_research_directions.md).

A world model is useful when choosing among possible futures matters. Static detection, retrieval, scheduling, or a calibrated analytical controller may already solve much of a product problem. The added cost of prediction must earn its place through improved decisions.

**Evidence scale:** published experiment, official product, provider deployment report, or proposed application. A product announcement is not an independently audited physical-accuracy result. The architectures and resource assessments below are proposed designs unless a source is named.

## Three evidence anchors

**Driving:** Waymo's February 6, 2026 official article describes a Genie-3-based driving world model with camera/lidar outputs and controls from prompts, driving inputs, and layouts. This is an organization-reported system and simulation use case. It does not provide the independent task-level validation needed to infer safety benefits from the demo alone. [Waymo World Model](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/02/the-waymo-world-model-a-new-frontier-for-autonomous-driving-simulation/).

**Virtual robot evaluation:** OSCAR's June 2026 preprint reports embodiment-conditioned video modeling and a relationship between generated-world policy evaluations and real outcomes. This makes simulation-to-real ranking correlation an interesting metric, but the claim must be examined per tested policy and embodiment rather than generalized to pure virtual certification. [OSCAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04463).

**Training-time future supervision:** LeRobot's July 2026 v0.6.0 article includes policy integrations with differing uses of prediction. Some drop future imagination at inference. Therefore test-time visual rollouts are not necessary for every system benefiting from world-model learning. [LeRobot release](https://huggingface.co/blog/lerobot-release-v060).

## ELI27: an adoption test

Before building a product, compare the best conventional baseline to a model-assisted variant. Hold data access and control latency fixed. Measure improvement on costly decisions and failures, then estimate collection, training, deployment, and maintenance costs.

A credible commercial contribution may be a data-alignment tool, benchmark, calibration report, or failure diagnostic rather than a larger model. Domain-specific evaluation often matters more than visual novelty.

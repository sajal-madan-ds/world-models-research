# Your study plan

Aim for 6–8 hours each week: two short weekday sessions and one weekend block. Understanding the observation, action, and evaluation contract matters more than rushing through papers.

## Four habits

1. **Explain before you implement.** Use the glass-on-a-table example to check your understanding.
2. **Keep the baseline.** Constant velocity, known physics, persistence, and simple controllers reveal what learning actually adds.
3. **Record failures.** A trained model can still drift, collapse, ignore actions, or mislead a planner.
4. **Separate evidence.** A paper claim, your own measurement, and a research hypothesis are different things.

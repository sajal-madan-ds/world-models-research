## A learning path that builds on what you know

You already know Python, PyTorch, and transformers. This course builds the missing bridge: how visual observations become representations, how representations predict change, and how predictions support actions.

## How to study

Read a chapter, work through its knowledge checks, then run the companion experiment. Mark the chapter complete when you can explain its main ideas without looking. Use search and the glossary whenever a term is unfamiliar.

The [twelve-week plan](study-plan.md) fits around a full-time engineering job. If you want a quick practical start, open [the measured experiments](measured.md) and see what the small models actually did.
